# Opening and Closing Procedures

Opening (6:15 for 7:00 doors)
1. Turn on espresso machine and grinders; flush groups
2. Dial in the House Espresso Blend — 18 g in, 38 g out, 27–30 s
3. Log fridge temps (Health Inspection Checklist)
4. Receive pastry delivery and check against the order
5. Brew first batch of drip; set up cold brew station per the [[Cold Brew Program]]

Closing (5:00 doors, out by 5:45)
1. Backflush machine, soak portafilters
2. Clean grinders, empty hoppers into airtight bins
3. Log fridge temps, label anything opened
4. Count the till; drop goes in the safe
5. Sundays only: run the Inventory Process

New hires shadow two opens and two closes before working solo — part of the Barista Training Program.
