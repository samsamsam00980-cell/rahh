# Roasting Schedule
Updated: 2026-09-08 · Owner: Maya

We roast on the 12 kg Diedrich every **Tuesday and Friday**, 6:00–11:00, before the café gets busy enough to need Dev on the floor.

- Tuesday: House Espresso Blend (4 batches), single origins for retail bags (2–3 batches)
- Friday: House Espresso Blend (3 batches), decaf, and whatever the Subscription Box needs that week
- Wholesale Accounts are roasted to order on Friday and delivered Monday, so orders must be in by Thursday noon.

Rest times: espresso needs at least 5 days before it hits the grinder; filter is fine at 3.

Green stock comes from [[Green Coffee Sourcing]]. If a batch drops more than 2% below target weight loss, log it in the [[Equipment Maintenance Log]] — last time it was the exhaust fan, not the beans.
