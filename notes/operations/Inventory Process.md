# Inventory Process
Every **Sunday at close**, Dev counts:
- Milk (dairy, oat, almond) and cream
- Cups, lids, sleeves, cold cups
- Retail bags and labels
- Green coffee by lot (with humidity reading)
- Syrups and seasonal ingredients for the Seasonal Menu Autumn

Counts go in the shared sheet. Par levels: 40 gal dairy, 18 cases oat, 2,000 hot cups.

Orders go out Monday morning. Paper goods via Restaurant Depot; milk via Hillside Dairy (Tue/Fri drops).

Waste is logged separately — it has been running around 6% of food cost, target is under 4%. Most of it is day-old pastry; we are renegotiating quantities in the [[Pastry Supplier Agreement]].

Supplier Invoices get matched against Sunday counts before anything is paid.
