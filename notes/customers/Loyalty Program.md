# Loyalty Program
Current: paper punch card, 10th drink free. No data, lots of lost cards.

Plan: move to the POS loyalty module (Square) in November.
- 1 point per $1, 100 points = free drink
- Double points on the Seasonal Menu Autumn drinks in the first two weeks
- Subscribers to the Subscription Box get 50 points a month

Cost estimate: ~2.5% of sales in redemptions based on the punch card redemption rate.

The Customer Feedback Summary Q3 mentions loyalty 11 times — people want it on their phone.

Promote via Email Newsletter and at the [[Grand Reopening Event]] (sign-up table with a free cookie).
