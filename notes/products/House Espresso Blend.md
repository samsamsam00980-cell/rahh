# House Espresso Blend — "Harbor"
60% Colombia Huila washed / 40% Brazil Cerrado natural. Roasted to a medium, ~14.5% weight loss, dropped at 214°C.

Tasting notes on the bag: milk chocolate, toasted almond, cherry.

This is 70% of our roasted volume: all café espresso drinks, most Wholesale Accounts, and the "classic" option in the Subscription Box.

Recipe card: 18 g in, 38 g out, 27–30 s, 93°C.

Cost per lb roasted (green + loss + labor + bag): about $8.10. Retail 12 oz bag sells for $17, wholesale 5 lb is $58 — see [[Pricing Strategy]].

Customers in the Customer Feedback Summary Q3 asked for a darker option. Trial batch planned for October; if it works it becomes a second blend rather than changing Harbor.
