# Cold Brew Program
Batch: 5 lb coarse ground Brazil Cerrado + 5 gal filtered water, 18-hour steep in the Toddy, filtered twice. Yields ~3.5 gal concentrate. Dilute 1:1 for service.

Shelf life: 7 days refrigerated, labeled with brew date (Health Inspection Checklist requirement).

Sales: cold brew was 22% of summer drink sales. Drops to ~8% in winter.

Retail: 32 oz growlers of concentrate ($16) sell well at the farmers market and are an add-on in the Subscription Box.
