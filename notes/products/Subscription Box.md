# Subscription Box
Launched March 2026. Customers choose:
- Classic: 2 × 12 oz House Espresso Blend ($30/month)
- Explorer: 2 × single origin, rotating ($36/month)
- Add-on: cold brew concentrate growler (+$14)

Subscribers: 142 as of September (up from 97 in June). Churn ~6%/month, mostly after month 3.

Fulfillment: roasted Friday per the [[Roasting Schedule]], packed Saturday, shipped Monday via USPS. Shipping is our biggest margin leak — $7.80 average.

Retention ideas:
- Handwritten note + a sample of next month's coffee
- Loyalty Program points for subscribers
- Pause option instead of cancel (Website Redesign needs to support this)

Mentioned monthly in the Email Newsletter.
