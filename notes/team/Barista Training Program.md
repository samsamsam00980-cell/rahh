# Barista Training Program
Owner: Priya. Length: 3 weeks for a new hire.

Week 1 — Foundations
- Food handler card (required, for the Health Inspection Checklist)
- Opening and Closing Procedures: shadow 2 opens, 2 closes
- Drip, cold brew, register

Week 2 — Espresso
- Dialing in the House Espresso Blend
- Milk steaming and latte art basics
- Workflow on a busy bar

Week 3 — Service
- Seasonal Menu Autumn drinks
- Handling complaints, Loyalty Program sign-ups
- Solo shifts with a lead on the floor

Sign-off: Priya checks 10 drinks in a row to spec.
